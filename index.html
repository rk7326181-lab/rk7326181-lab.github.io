Cluster Payout Optimization

A geospatial cost model that maps every delivery-cluster polygon and its surge rate, finds the hubs where payouts are leaking money, and recommends the cheapest correction.

Problem

  • Riders earn an INR 0–14 surge per delivery, set by the polygon the delivery falls in.
  • Clustering incentives cost about INR 10M a month, and where a polygon's rate didn't match its distance from the hub, cost per order rose unnoticed.

Approach

  • Daily BigQuery refresh through GitHub Actions.
  • Haversine distances from hub to polygon, plus an SOP-compliance check.
  • Point-in-polygon matching of shipments to compute burn per polygon and a priority score.
  • Anomaly detection (Isolation Forest, LOF) to flag unusual hubs.

Outcome

  • INR 1.6–1.7M a month saved (≈ US$18–20K), 80–85% of an INR 2.0M monthly target.
  • Ranked, explainable actions for operations ("lower the rate or widen the radius"), each with before/after cost.

DE Rider MIS: Delivery Executive cost & productivity

An automated reporting system that picks up a daily rider file of about 950K rows from Slack, keeps only the Delivery Executive riders, applies approved payout rules and serves managers a role-based dashboard, built entirely on free Google tools.

01 · DailySlack upload~950K-row export
02 · LocalStreaming filter492 MB → ~3 MB
03 · RulesApproved formulasCPO · MG · proration · burn
04 · ServeRole-based dashboardRider · hub · city · month

Problem

  • DE cost and productivity reporting was done by hand in Excel.
  • Daily exports of 146–186 MB, of which only about 0.6% of rows were DE riders, and no history was kept.

Approach

  • Slack intake with file-level and row-level de-duplication.
  • A streaming pre-filter to stay within Apps Script's limits.
  • A rules engine that matches the Excel formulas exactly, with formulas in the sheet that managers can edit.
  • A metric only goes live once its formula is approved.

Outcome

  • Live for 780+ riders and about 500K orders a week.
  • Every figure traces back to an approved, auditable formula.
  • Zero running cost; August 2026 fully backfilled (15,054 rider-days).

Geo Intelligence Portal

A six-step pipeline that builds distance-based payout polygons around each hub and compares per-shipment profit and loss between postal-code-level and cluster-level payout, replacing a workflow split across Jupyter, Google My Maps and Excel.

Problem

  • Each clustering run took 4–5 hours across three separate tools.

Approach

  • Haversine or OSRM road distances assign payout slabs.
  • Concentric rings clipped to postal-code boundaries, with a drag-to-edit map editor.
  • A spatial index matches 60 days of shipments to polygons.

Outcome

  • 30–60 minutes per run, about 85% faster.
  • One tool instead of three, with CSV, Excel and KML exports.
